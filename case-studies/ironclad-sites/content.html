Ironclad Sites builds lead-generating websites for contractors, service businesses, real estate professionals, and property managers. I handled both the full website build and the SEO strategy for their own site, the same site they use to sell web design services to their clients, so it needed to hold up to real scrutiny.

The challenge

A web design agency's own website is its best (or worst) advertisement. If Ironclad Sites is selling "fast, conversion-focused websites" to contractors, their own site needs to actually be fast, well-optimized, and technically sound, or it undercuts the pitch immediately.

What I did

  • Built the full WordPress site with a conversion-focused layout: clear hero messaging, pricing, portfolio, and booking flow
  • Implemented ProfessionalService and WebSite schema markup for structured data
  • Optimized on-page SEO: meta descriptions, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs
  • Tuned performance across both mobile and desktop

Results

Tested with Google PageSpeed Insights and Rich Results Test: 97 performance and 100 SEO on both mobile and desktop, with 3 valid structured data items detected and zero critical issues.

Tools used

WordPress, Elementor, Google PageSpeed Insights, Google Rich Results Test, on-page SEO best practices.
